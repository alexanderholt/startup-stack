# Compliance Review Checklist

> **This is a template.** Copy it to `checklist.md` in this folder and replace
> the example items with your own industry-specific compliance items.
> `checklist.md` is gitignored, so your real checklist stays local.
> Each item should be a single verifiable claim that can be rated against a specific regulation.

Review the subject against each item below. For each, cite the specific regulation and rate: ✅ Compliant, ⚠️ Risk, ❌ Non-compliant, or ❓ Insufficient info.

## Core regulatory requirements
- [ ] [The product meets requirement X under regulation Y]
- [ ] [Required registrations or licenses are held in each jurisdiction served]
- [ ] [Required filings are current]

## Conflicts of interest and compensation
- [ ] Compensation from partners or vendors is disclosed where required
- [ ] No undisclosed compensation could influence what users are shown

## User-facing language and disclosures
- [ ] Required disclosures are present and accurate
- [ ] Terminology matches regulatory definitions

## Data handling and privacy
- [ ] User data handling complies with applicable privacy regulations
- [ ] Data sharing with third parties is limited to what the user consents to
- [ ] Data retention and deletion policies are documented

## Marketing claims
- [ ] Claims about the product are accurate and verifiable
- [ ] No claims of regulatory endorsement or approval unless actually granted
- [ ] Comparative claims are substantiated
